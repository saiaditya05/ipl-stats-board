              Team names follow the file: Delhi Daredevils shows as Delhi Capitals, Kings XI Punjab as Punjab Kings, Royal Challengers Bangalore as Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Deccan Chargers (2008 to 2012) and Sunrisers Hyderabad (2013 on) share one team id in the file and are separated by season. The 2020 season was played in the UAE and is listed as 2020. Wickets credit the bowler only for caught, bowled, lbw, stumped, caught and bowled and hit wicket. Super overs are left out of player and scoring numbers. For the 31 matches after 1 May 2026, winners and margins come from the ball by ball file (checked against the published league table), so a run margin can be off by a run or two, and toss and player of the match are not available.
